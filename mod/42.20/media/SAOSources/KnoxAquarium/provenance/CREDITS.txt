CHANGES MADE TO THE ORIGINAL MODEL
The original .glb was not modified or redistributed. It was used as a rendering
source only. For this mod the glass tank was isolated from the wider scene
(table, floor, room lighting and several unused props were discarded), its
materials were brightened for Project Zomboid's flat isometric lighting, and a
new charcoal cabinet, plinth, doors, handles, fitted lid, feeding hatch, water
volume and waterline were modelled procedurally around it in Blender. The four
swimming fish, their motion paths and every user-interface icon are original
work created for this mod. Everything shipped in this mod is a set of 2D sprites
rendered from that scene; no mesh, texture or material file from the original
download is included.

OPTIONAL COMPATIBILITY - FISHING OVERHAUL BR
Knox Aquarium detects "Fishing Overhaul BR [B42]" (Workshop 3784235810, mod id
PeixesBRExpansion, by gadinho) and works with its species when it is installed.

Its fish are found by reading the base game's own fishing registry, and the
Inspect window shows its 3D models by asking each fish item for the model name
it already carries - those models and textures stay that mod's own files,
loaded from that mod, and used only while it is installed.

SPECIES SPRITES (since 0.13). This mod ships 2D sprite frames of 97 of his
species swimming, used in the Large and Display tanks. They were rendered, with
gadinho's permission as a partner in this project, from the 3D models and
textures in his mod. Only the rendered 2D frames are included - no mesh,
texture or model file of his is shipped. Many of those species come from the
"Lake Fish Pack" and "River Fish Pack" by TricksUp, which are licensed to him.
The species only appear while his mod is installed, because only his fish can
be put in a tank to show them.

Neither mod requires the other. With Knox Aquarium alone every tank works and
draws its own aquarium fish.

DISPLAY AQUARIUM MODEL
The display aquarium is rendered from a 3D aquarium model by CGAxis
(cgaxis.com, model "cgaxis_models_24_09"). As with every tank here, the model
is not shipped: only 2D sprite frames rendered from it. Its built-in shoal of
fish was removed; the cabinet, lid and water were built for this mod.

LARGE AQUARIUM MODEL
The large aquarium ("Paludario") is based on a 3D model built by gadinho, author
of Fishing Overhaul BR, and given to this mod when he stopped work on his own
aquarium mod. It originates from "Paludarium Vivarium Aquarium #1", purchased by
him on Fab; he has confirmed it may be used here.

The model is not shipped as geometry. It was converted, its glass faces removed,
and rendered to 2D sprite frames in the same Blender pipeline used for every
other tank in this mod - which is why the large tank costs about 9 MB rather
than the 88 MB the raw meshes would have added. The arapaima swimming in it is
his model too, extracted from the same file.
