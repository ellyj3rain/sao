## Two things that are decided, so do not ask

**Publishing.** A closed batch reaches `origin/main` through a branch
and a pull request that you merge yourself. Never push to `main`; it is
protected and will refuse you. The shape is in `NEO.md` under
Publishing. Do not ask the operator what shape to use, and do not leave
the pull request open for them to find.

**The model.** This project is built after `../colonist-awareness`
(CAO). When something about process is unclear, read how CAO did it and
do that. Replicate first. If a piece genuinely cannot transfer, say so
and stop - do not substitute a design of your own and call it
equivalent.
