# Thread index

Thread families and threads are the permanent, series-neutral classification
over the batch sequence: they connect related work across nonadjacent batches
without changing chronological order. A batch may belong to several threads.
Thread identifiers (`T-*`) and family identifiers (`TF-*`) classify work; batch
identifiers record it; version units in [`../VERSION_MAP.md`](../VERSION_MAP.md)
partition contiguous development time.

This index was rebuilt at the recatalog: the former index described clusters
left behind by the diary rather than the work's threads, and its batch lists
were illustrative rather than complete. Every batch is now classified.
