## Record

The overnight rotation that hardened the standing machinery. Subsystems and
individual agents sit behind their own fault gates so one bad seam can no
longer sink the ship while nobody watches; the scope-split scanner became a
habit. The audits paid immediately: a house could be blocked from its own
claim edge by its own belief (F-031); the voice surface was covered for every
reachable key; dead memories stopped decaying (F-033) and belief entry stopped
respecting lapsed claims (F-034); the county menu could not reach the
inhabitants at all (F-032 - under-cursor verbs walked only our registry). The
economy deepened honestly: smoke-for-bread trade, the import of Knox trust at
half-strength, memorial revisits on long cadences. The Ledger became an
artifact: factions with creed and ground and wars, the recent dead, and peace
when two leaders' regard heals past the line - nothing rolled, all surfaces
spoken. The county's whole past has voices, including the other mod's buried
read read-only from their store. Then the feud gained its opposite number's
twin and the houses learned to schism: internal mutual hostility splits a
company at the meeting seam along the trust fault line, the estranged core
walks out into a new name and new ground, an exile of one is not a rival
house, and dormant meeting points became election moments so a divided house
can break on the road. Feud history timestamps onto both sides; the Chronicle
renders the wars.
