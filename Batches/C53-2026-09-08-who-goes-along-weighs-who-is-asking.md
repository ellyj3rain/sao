## Record

Day Zero slice 4. [B19] built the joining - a venture is announced,
the hearers each decide from who they are, the keenest go, and three
caps bind - and it weighed everything about the hearer and nothing
about the trip or the caller. [C49] named the first of those gaps
while deleting an authority table elsewhere and deferred it here.

**Who is asking.** The pull gains the office the caller holds over
this person, taken from `SAO_Command`'s own ladder: a leader's call
0.40 above a peer's, a second's or a proven hand's 0.25 above. No
number is invented at the site. `officeOf` rather than `standingOf`,
because standing folds trust in and trust is already the pull's first
term - this adds the office alone. An invitation is not an order and
does not go through the gate's verdict, but the office is the same
social fact either way and DR-033 says it is the houses, their
leaders and their designations that carry a word. The divided house
arrives with it for free: the chair of a house at war with itself
calls to the half leaning away with a peer's voice.

**What is being asked.** A warpath is not a bread run, and the site
already half knew it - the wall is abandoned for a raid and for
nothing else, which is the line directly above. It never asked the
person being invited. It asks now, through the envelope
`SAO_Command` uses for the same question before an order to engage:
unarmed, past the fear a child carries, or the disposition's own
refusal of that fight, and they stay home. A refusal and not a
weight, because somebody who will not take a fight is not persuaded
into one by liking you.

**Ported to the mirror in the same batch**, which is that script's own
doctrine. `joining_test` reimplements the live gates in Python over
the converged 60-person county; both changes went in with it,
including deriving each house's leader and second the way Standing
derives them - the member with the highest trust-sum from the others,
which is the sum the election runs on - so the office here is the same
fact and not a stand-in.

**And the mirror refused the first claim made about it.** The
assertion written first compared the average party a leader takes
against the average a peer takes, and answered 1.14 against 1.68 -
the office apparently costing company. It was a bad measurement:
leaders are not in the same houses, with the same hearers and the
same circle caps, as the peers being compared with. Isolating the
term instead - the same hearer, the same trip, one term removed -
gave a clearer and worse answer: **the office carried nobody at all,
in 690 invitations.**

That is F-056, and it is the real result of this batch. Measured on
the mirror's own county: the pull's median is 1.28 against a
threshold of 0.55, and 96% of invitations clear it before any of the
pull's terms are consulted. Housemate trust saturates as the county
converges, so the dominant term takes the sum far past the line on
its own and nothing else in the calculation can decide anything. Who
comes along is settled by the three refusals and then by the caps.
Two things follow and neither is taken here: the threshold is an
authored number and moving it changes how every house feels, and the
saturation is the equilibrium mirror's own convergence as much as the
county's.

So the mirror gained a sixth sweep - a house that formed recently,
with trust scaled down, labelled a stand-in exactly as
`needful_share` and the wall share already are. There the threshold
is the one that binds, and the office carries 46 hearers who would
otherwise have stayed home. All of them are in that sweep and none
anywhere else, which is both the term working and F-056 restated.
That house is the house a day-zero county is made of, which is why
the slice is worth having.

**Not in this batch.** A pull that discriminates in a settled county,
which needs the threshold moved and is a design call. Vehicles as
composition, which is the slice after this one.
