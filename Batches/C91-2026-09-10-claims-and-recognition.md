## Record

The operator corrected the organization contract: some social facts are not
self-declared. A person may claim an office, membership, a resource, a role,
or a bond, but the claim is separate from its recognition.

The contract now records the claim, the people or institution that recognize
it, and the affected person's response. Coercion can make a claim effective
without making it legitimate.

The governance labels are analytical, not speech lines. No character says `I am
despotic` or `I am localist`. A character says what they do or expect: `I decide
here`, `we vote`, `the house decides`, or `this ground is ours`.

## What changed here

- `ORGANIZATION.md` gains a `Claims and recognition` section.
- `ORGANIZATION.md` states that governance names describe the shape of
  authority and are not self-descriptions.
- `REPRESENTATION.md` now asks what claim is being made and who recognizes it.
