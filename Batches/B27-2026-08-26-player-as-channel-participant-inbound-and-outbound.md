## Record

The operator's law - one experience loop, the only distinction being direction
of communication - was audited end to end. The inbound violation: every tell
took an NPC id, so the player could never put a belief into anyone's head.
The fix was not a player pathway (that would be two loops): the perception,
store, and tell functions were already generic over the id, and the player IS
an IsoPlayer with an id and a body like anyone else; the player perceives
through the same function on the same interval into the same store, and tells
through "Tell them what I've seen" - with `chosen` skipping only the speaker's
reticence, never the listener's skepticism. The canonical playerKey
constructor was created (fifteen hand-spellings recorded as known duplication
and deliberately not churned in a channel-altering batch). Outbound: wire
listening became one definition with two callers, what crosses is identical,
and what a bulletin does to your interior stays yours; the ledger and
management surfaces were scoped out with the real tension named for the
operator. Subject: survivors already perceived the player - verified, not
assumed. The mirror then made [B27]'s claim mechanical: the same bulletin
lands identically for survivor and player keys, and the pre-fix behavior is
reproduced by demand.
