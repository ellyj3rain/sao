## Record

DR-022, built on the verified body law rather than on hope: SAO
absorbs the neighbour framework's people completely. At world start
every living profile in his store becomes a county record; his
zombie-backed body is handed off through HIS OWN teardown pair
(DetachRuntimeActor + RemoveActorShell) - removed, never killed,
because his grief and memorial machinery hangs on the death event
removal does not fire (F-051). From then on the person is ours:
perception, standing, movement, combat, death, and the turn.

**The one door, held.** His `SpawnActor` is the single body-creation
choke point for every path he has - restores, encounters, the spouse,
beacon visitors, away-team returns - and his restore loop re-bodies a
missing person within ten in-game minutes (F-051). The wrap returns
nil for absorbed people, absorbs NEW profiles at birth (so his
encounters keep happening as events, DR-023 - what walks out of them
is one of ours), and falls back to HIS spawn when absorption itself
fails, because a person existing his way beats a person existing no
way.

**Taken whole.** The record carries his name, his archetype label as
a presumed trade ([A22]'s class), his position - and his durable
inventory ledger translated into the county's own hibernation pack,
so SAO's existing materialize-and-awaken machinery dresses and equips
the person with what they actually carried (nesting flattens; the
items survive). His world-group members keep their company through
the county's standing machinery under a `ksg:` group id (the county
elects its own leader - his may differ, stated); his player-squad
members arrive trusting the player who recruited them.

**Truth mirrored back.** His rows are never deleted - by him or by us
- and his surfaces keep reading them. The county writes position back
while an absorbed person lives and `alive = false` once when they
die, so his roster does not lie about people who are now ours. His
death ceremonies do not run for them; the county's own grief carries
that weight.

**The dials, blocked in his engine's own idiom.** His two population
caps would strangle his encounter stream at 18 forever once everyone
is absorbed (his living count still sees his rows), so they are
neutralized at his one option seam (`GetOption`, F-051) - and BLOCKED
on the sandbox screen with vanilla's own grey-and-disable idiom
(F-050), tooltip in plain words (DR-018): "Overridden by Survivor
Awareness: the county sizes its own population. Encounter settings
still apply."

**The menu is not the behavior (DR-024).** His people answered
"regroup" and "unstick"; absorbed people do too - "regroup on me" and
"get unstuck" on the Ask-them-to menu, the county's machinery
underneath (the unstick verb got the public bridge hand Lua could not
otherwise reach). Follow, wait, return-home, the card, introduce,
recruit, and offer already existed under the county's labels.

**The border.** Border 98 (`tools/absorb_test.py`): the wrap with its
honest fallback, the start sweep, remove-never-kill (scanned on
comment-stripped source - its own first run tripped on the module's
prose naming the event it exists to avoid), no marker keys written,
the mirror back, the blocked dials, and verb parity. Control: the
pre-[C20] tree.

**Named limits, stated rather than discovered:** his per-actor
systems (his combat, his squad behavior) no longer apply to absorbed
people - the DR-022 cost, accepted; bag nesting flattens in the
inventory carry; his roster renders absorbed people as unloaded
rather than absent; and the whole batch awaits its first live receipt
like everything else on the ledger.

This governed record is the portable project history for this unit.
