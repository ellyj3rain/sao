# Offline acceptance receipts (pre-recatalog)

Per CAO's acceptance-receipt idiom: what ran, what it proved, what it
cannot prove. Play receipts (live-world observations) remain each
batch's own pending list; these are the receipts generable offline.

> **[B34] correction.** When these results were first recorded the
> three scripts lived only in a session temp directory and were never
> committed - the proof below could not be re-run by anyone. They now
> live in `tools/` and run together via `tools/mirrors.sh` (named
> `trilogy.sh` until [B35] added a fourth mirror).

## Trilogy run - 2026-08-26, tree at [B4]-complete

- **depth_test_c3.py: PASS.** Individual pasts remain class-true
  after the era's history changes (DayZero lesson gating, dated
  lessons): hardened 5/5, carer 4/4, outdoors 7/7, settled 10/11,
  trades 12/13 class-appropriate; lived-shares sane per class.
- **census_test.py: PASS.** The labor distribution holds: common
  trades numerous (unemployed/trucker/construction/farmhand 19-20),
  rares rare (police 2, doctor 1, delta 1), no absent common rows -
  no hash pathology.
- **equilibrium_test.py: PASS.** The society converges at 120 days:
  17 companies sized 2-4, 6 deliberate loners persisting (the A189
  circle law), 8 feuds declared / 4 peaces / 12 hostile pairs -
  living politics, no mega-company, no universal feud.

## What these prove / cannot prove

Prove: the social-law core (trust, meetings, joins gated by circle,
creeds, feuds, peace, schism pressure, personal hostility bars) still
produces a living county; the census generator still draws the real
distribution; generated pasts still fit their classes. Cannot prove:
anything body-side (scanner sight, actions, combat, farming, vehicle
seats, bites) - those are play receipts by nature.

  mirror has no absence/search machinery; the warm is conditional on
  a search moment that cannot occur in it. Bounded, rare, stated.
- B3 bitten wary drip (-0.02/day per pair): OUT OF MODEL - the mirror
  has no body states; bites are transient and the drip is day-capped.
  Stated, not ported.

  entirely (it models standing, not jobs). Stated.
