## Record

[C128] lived catch-up on the live 240-tick cadence. The comments around that loop still described a year as a simulation, and still said the pass runs once per simulated day. [C112] derived the tick from one real hour of wall-clock per game day. On this build the default DayLength is one hour and thirty minutes of real time per twenty-four game hours, and the setting is variable.

The operator ruled that collapsing days into years as a unit of simulation does not make sense. 365 days is 365 days. Simulating a year is simulating 365 days. Game time inside a day is not wall-clock time.

[C129] names the three clocks in `SAO_History`:

- Calendar. A year is 365 days. Catch-up lives days. The persisted keys `yearsOwed` and `yearsRun` still count days; the names are [C45]'s save keys. There is no year-step.
- Game time. A day is 24 game hours. A tick is 1/9000 of a game hour. [C128] already lives this.
- Wall clock. Real time per game day is the engine's DayLength. Default 1.5 hours. Variable. The DayLength enum is not decoded here: the jar is not in this tree.

The 9000 stays. It is the game-hour quantum, not a frame count at the wrong default. Span constants keep their numbers. Old-age risk divides the life table by `DAYS_PER_YEAR`. FastSimulation stays opt-in interpolation of a curve; it is not a year of simulation.
