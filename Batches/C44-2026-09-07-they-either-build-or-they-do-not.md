## Record

The operator ruled through Crucible on what must exist before the
years between 1993 and a later start are simulated, and the answer
rejected the question's framing: nothing is forced. The county is not
handed settlements and no pass authors them. The people are given what
they need and either they manage it or they do not, and a county that
never manages it is a finding about the systems rather than a reason
to place a fortification by hand.

So this batch builds a capability, not an outcome, and it starts with
the smallest thing that is really building: putting a plank across a
window.

**The act is the game's own.** `SAOBuild` calls
`IsoBarricade.AddBarricadeToObject` and `addPlank` - the same calls
the player's own barricade action makes - and it wants exactly what
that action's `isValid` wants, read off the shipped Lua and checked
against the jar: a hammer-tagged tool equipped, a plank equipped, two
`Base.Nails` in the bag, and a window that is not already full.

**It is paid for.** The plank and both nails leave their inventory. A
barricade that costs nothing is a decoration, and a county that can
fortify for free tells us nothing about whether it could gather what
it needed - which is the only thing worth learning here.

**Every clause can fail.** They must hold ground, the fall must have
come to the county ([C42]), they must be standing on their own claim
so nobody boards up somebody else's house, they must be carrying
materials they found themselves, and there must be a window left. A
decision that cannot fail is a placement wearing a decision's clothes.

**What the county says about it.** The panel reports the windows a
person has actually boarded, and says nothing for everyone who never
managed one - which is the honest reading of a county that has not.
The voice has a line for the moment.

**Border 117** cannot check that anything was built, because that is
the outcome and the outcome is the measurement. It checks the
capability is real, honest and unfaked: the engine's own calls, the
shipped action's own requirements, the materials genuinely leaving the
bag, every clause of the decision present, and - the check the ruling
exists for - that no genesis pass, no population pass, no absorption
and no harness option places a barricade. A later batch that quietly
seeded what it wanted to see would destroy the only measurement there
is, and this is what would catch it.

**Not in this batch.** Everything else a person might build: walls,
fences, furniture, repairs, the second plank being a different
decision from the first. Burial and graves. Trade between places. A
settlement as a place rather than a claim with stores. The years
between simulated forward, which is what this capability exists to be
measured by.

This governed record is the portable project history for this unit.
