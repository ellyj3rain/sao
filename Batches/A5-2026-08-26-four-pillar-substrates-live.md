## Record

The four pillars became code. Perception: a facing-cone plus occlusion scanner
returning one compact string to Lua (no engine objects cross), beliefs with
provenance and decay, and the deliberate distinction between "believes clear"
and "has not looked". Disposition: deterministic per-id traits bounded to the
human envelope, decisions as preferences with latency. Standing: relations,
groups, territory claims, and permission checks over what a survivor may enter
or engage. Controller: the composition running, decisions consuming only
Perception queries, Standing gating goals, Execution acting. Movement's
repeating Lua-side failure class (engine objects Kahlua cannot handle) was
eliminated by transplanting the route supervision Java-side; Lua locomotion
became a thin verdict consumer, and live walk verification was deliberately
parked rather than gated on.

This governed record is the portable project history for this unit. Anything it
was found to have asserted against later corrections is corrected in place above
with the correction cited; the raw entries remain reachable through
[`FORMER_LABELS.md`](FORMER_LABELS.md).
