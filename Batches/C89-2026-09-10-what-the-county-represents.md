## Record

The operator set the representation target after the trades-hinge proposal
landed. The county is a life simulation under survival pressure. Work comes
from pressure, person, relationship, and material state. Organization comes
after a repeated pattern exists. A survivor may know of nobody and want nobody
nearby, and that state is a complete behavior mode.

This batch writes that target as a representation contract.

The gate itself needed one repair: `tools/check.sh` called
`tools/undeclared_audit.py` with the hard-coded command `python` while the rest
of the gate used `$PY`, which resolves to `python3` on a machine without a
`python` command. The border now uses the same interpreter variable as every
other tool.

## What changed here

`REPRESENTATION.md` is the new canonical contract. It states what the game
represents, what each existing state surface carries, what each surface may
cause, and what it may never cause. It names `designation` as the one
overconcentrated surface: a work word should describe repeated work after it
happens, and today the same string also causes controller behavior, study
choices, rationing, and command standing.

The contract also states the labor target. Labor is broader than a fixed job
list. Ease is a valid state, because rebuilding requires surplus, choice, and
work that is not immediately forced by survival.

The document also states the dataset consequence: a row records the person,
pressure, available means, relationships, culture and politics, and the action
taken. The label is the next action. Survival is the setting, not the reward.

The version machine rolled the patch coordinate, and the shipped jar was
rebuilt from the bumped `VERSION` so the gate's jar-version border stayed clean.

## No new border

This batch changes documents and one line in the gate. No behavior under
`mod/` changes, and no new instrument is added. The gate still runs over the
