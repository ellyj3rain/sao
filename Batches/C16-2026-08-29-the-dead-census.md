## Record

DR-021's mechanism B, first half: before any zombie-population number
is recommended, the install's actual crowd is MEASURED - because the
absolute count behind a sandbox multiplier lives in a native library
no structural pass can read (the sibling's F-006), and a
recommendation without a measurement is the bite formula's sin with
bigger numbers.

**The instrument.** `deadCensus` on the bridge reads four raw numbers
through javap-verified surfaces: the fungible crowd and the
identity-bearing bodies in the loaded area (split by [C9]'s
predicate, so a living neighbour or a risen known face is never
counted as crowd), the loaded area's tile extent (IsoChunkMap's
public tile bounds), and the installed map's extent (IsoMetaGrid
cells times `IsoCell.getCellSizeInSquares()`). It derives nothing and
changes nothing - the [C6] discipline, held by the border's inertness
check.

**Where the numbers go.** The telemetry daily line gains a
`deadcensus` event: the raw fields plus density per thousand loaded
tiles and a whole-map projection whose sampling assumption is stated
in the same comment that makes the number - the loaded area
oversamples wherever the player lingers, so ratification reads many
lines across sessions, never one. The inspect panel shows the live
split in plain copy ("zombies loaded: N (+M identity-bearing)").

**What this sets up.** With a measured zombies-per-multiplier picture
on the operator's own install, GROUNDED_DEAD_PROPOSAL.md's derived
total (~8,000-13,500) becomes a concrete multiplier recommendation
for the operator to ratify, and the presence layer (DR-021's second
half, the next batch's work) gets a baseline its ledger can reconcile
against - the state agreement with [B21]'s pool-taking starts from a
number somebody actually counted.

**The border.** Border 95 (`tools/dead_census_test.py`): the verb and
its four verified reads, the inertness ban (no setter, removal, or
spawn in the body), the telemetry fields with the stated assumption,
and the panel row. Control: the pre-[C16] tree, which fails four
ways.
