## Record

The county gained a medium. A survivor-run transmitter - "The County Wire",
101.2 - is built strictly on vanilla's own DynamicRadio plug-in pattern
(exemplar-verified; the unverifiable SendTransmission was refused), airing
hourly bulletins composed at air time from the claims behind each item:
elections, policy turns, schisms, feuds, peace, and every death through the
one markDead funnel; dead air interrupted by a lifeline beacon; the frequency
traveling by word of mouth. Then the loop closed: the player's voice goes
physically out on the band when they carry a live two-way radio (the engine's
own physical requirements via OnAddMessage and DeviceData), keeping houses
hear it (warmed trust, remembered), and the wire answers back. Politics crossed
house lines: complementary houses' leaders shake on bread-for-watch pacts with
passage rights and a betrayer's wound; the pact is kept by real deliverers
walking real bread and real watch legs; and the player's calls over the wire
(call for aid as a collectible debt, share your camp as told-provenance
beliefs, ask for the news on demand) direct the county at radio range. Unpaid
calls get harder as debt accrues - the band is not a bell you hammer.
