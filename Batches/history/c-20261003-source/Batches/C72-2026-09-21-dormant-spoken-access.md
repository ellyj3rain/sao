## Measured contract

C67 admitted an adjacent dormant conversation only when both people had measured
hearing and an explicit awake state. Body snapshots preserved native fatigue and
endurance, and PhysicalFacts observed sleep, but no bodyless producer advanced
rest, sleep or wake. A sleeping record could therefore remain asleep forever;
an older record with no sleep observation remained correctly unknown but had no
route to acquire current state.

The [producer matrix](../artifacts/audits/20260921-2224Z-1524PST-dormant-spoken-access/PLAN.md)
separates native measurement, durable advancement, spoken admission and radio
reception. Colonist Awareness checks the engine pawn's current awake state and
has no bodyless-person transition to transfer; Survivor Awareness requires this
record-owned seam because its engine body is temporary.

## Implemented behavior

The native snapshot bridge reads fatigue, endurance and saved sleep-need traits
directly from a validated v3/v4 envelope without constructing a body. Snapshot
commit records those measurements and their county hour. New generated people
receive explicit initial provenance. Older records that have only the prior
speech-access marker remain unknown until a supported native capture supplies
measurements.

`SAO_DormantPopulation` advances that state on the existing whole-population
pass. The county clock and installed fatigue rate drive awake accumulation.
At-home people rest from 22:00 to 06:00, sleep after the existing fatigue
threshold, recover through the same charged law used by loaded non-player
sleepers and wake at the morning boundary. Sleeping people do not take the
dormant movement step. The transition completes before dormant encounters, so
the existing Communication admission reads produced awake/asleep state rather
than a default.

Body restoration applies the completed fatigue, endurance, sleep and seated
posture after native restoration and verifies each state before publication.
Controller adoption resumes an already sleeping person without charging the
dormant interval again. The loaded sleep path continues until full recovery or
the morning boundary, matching the bodyless state machine.

## Verification

Border 185 executes the production Kahlua owner through sleep at 22:00,
movement exclusion, encounter refusal, morning wake, encounter admission,
native-state acquisition, exact awake accumulation and both saved sleep traits.
Its legacy case carries the earlier generated speech marker and remains unknown.
Three mutations remove the sleep write, default legacy state and remove recovery;
each flips its named result. Two static controls reverse scheduler order and
remove the post-restore overlay.

The native-person probe reads exact snapshot rest values, restores them and
applies a later bodyless interval. The person-handoff suite executes fatigue,
endurance, sleep and seated-posture replacement after native restoration and
rejects missing overlay/posture seams. The tick-walk border holds dormant life
at its prior one full-store pass. The [evidence record](../artifacts/audits/20260921-2224Z-1524PST-dormant-spoken-access/README.md)
holds the focused and full-gate results.

## Limits and continuation

C72 establishes whether an adjacent dormant person can participate in spoken
delivery or request testimony at that county hour. It does not establish radio
reception: a receiver, tuned channel and recipient access still need a real
acquisition producer. Main-menu startup proves packaging and loadability;
loaded-save play acceptance remains separate. The next coupled work is actual
radio acquisition, followed by the selected R12 source/acquisition example.
