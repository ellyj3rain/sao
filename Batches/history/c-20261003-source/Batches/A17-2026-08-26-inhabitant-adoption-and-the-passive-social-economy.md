## Record

The inhabitants entered the economy without being replaced. Each record's past
now rides a split clock - world months scaled by a per-person contact factor,
so a hermit's year settles less than a trader's fortnight - with provenance
rolled at generation (lived, witnessed, told, at their proper weights) and
names only on lived mortal claims; the operator's depth gate passed on
measured evidence (33 distinct claim sets among 40, and 65% of claim-sharing
pairs disagreeing on provenance - one paid, one heard, they can argue). A live
Knox human seen in the world gets a record keyed to their profile id, seeded
from the engine's own hours-survived with a contact-scaled past; our
controller never drives them, but they stand in the exchange and initiate it,
so lessons, trust, and company move both ways. The hardening followed: no
body is ever conjured for a Knox record (F-024); dormant road meetings became
full conversations both directions, making inhabitants knowledge carriers;
the audit of the exchange against passive initiators confirmed body-gated
blocks no-op and record-side blocks run; and mourning was unified so an
inhabitant's death is witnessed, judged, and grieved by the same law as ours -
killers named, witnesses at the body walking over, the bonded minting their
fork. Their own camp seeds their knowledge from their store, read-only.
