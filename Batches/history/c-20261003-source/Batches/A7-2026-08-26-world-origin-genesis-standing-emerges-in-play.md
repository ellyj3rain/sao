## Record

Genesis stopped being a refill effect around the player - the exact thing
ARCHITECTURE.md forbids - and started drawing from the map's real spawn-region
point tables, region-balanced, at real houses in real towns wherever the
player happens to be. Every policy number became a sandbox option with
player-facing copy, and the refill clock runs on world-age hours. On the same
ground: a survivor's origin house is their address, with dusk sending the
unthreatened home; a health drop names its author through getAttackedBy, and
being attacked by a person collapses them into hostile standing; hostile
people weigh in the threat decision beside zombies at the survivor's own
nerve distance; and trust accrues slowly from peaceful proximity - slow
enough to mean something. Stand-or-flee became a per-person choice: the armed
and willing engage through the combat loop while others flee; everyone
carries what their traits imply at first meeting; warnings flow along trust
or membership; and two strangers whose mutual trust crosses the line begin
keeping company - a group born from encounters, never from a menu.
