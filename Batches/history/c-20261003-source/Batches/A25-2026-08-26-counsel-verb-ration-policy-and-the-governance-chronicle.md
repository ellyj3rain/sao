## Record

Governance at scale, in both directions between player and county. Counsel is
earned authority: an accepted member standing with their leader may urge peace
with a named enemy or urge settling - moves dispositions, never pulls levers.
And the commons: a group's meaning changes with size - a band shares
personally, a company has jobs and a quartermaster's stores, a community (8+)
answers who eats first by its creed (watch-first, house-first, weak-first,
carry-light honestly declared rather than performed), policy lapses below
eight, dissent drains the chair's trust and grumbles aloud at election
moments, and decisions leave traces in govHistory so a returning player
discovers what the county decided in their absence. This unit's record also
carries, verbatim, the operator's correction that defines a batch from then
on: a batch is a designed feature, not confetti commits relabeled as breadth.
The medium changed with it: the county report stopped being console prose and
became a window in the game's own idiom (ISCollapsableWindow), with the
Chronicle carrying the wars and the turns, and debug surfaces restructured
under the game's menu conventions.

This governed record is the portable project history for this unit. Anything it
was found to have asserted against later corrections is corrected in place above
