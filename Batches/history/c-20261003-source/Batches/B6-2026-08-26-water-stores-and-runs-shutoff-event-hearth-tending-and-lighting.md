## Record

The calendar became material. Water is the second axis: houses count stored
water beside the food with honestly-aging claims; dry houses run real water
errands (fill what is in reach, else walk and fill, carry home - the
deposit-water split was caught as an announced deposit that was never queued);
the day the mains stop is stamped, aired, and chronicled; stored water is
taken under the same ration policy as the shelves; water charity was refused
for having no honest seam. And fire: cold survivors walk to hearths (a need,
not an errand), feed them what burns by the engine's own getMinutesToBurn
truth, light dead fires only when carrying the means (the vanilla kindle
action wants a campfire object indoor hearths do not have - setLit behind a
real inventory requirement is the honest one, stated), severe cold wakes the
sleeper, warm houses soften winter's dormant edge, and the dark-hearth house
sends people out for firewood. B6's original sensor read - the engine's cold
illness mistaken for temperature - is corrected here on the record, with all
thresholds re-scaled.
