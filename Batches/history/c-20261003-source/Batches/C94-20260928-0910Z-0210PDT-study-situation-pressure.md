C94 makes survival pressure a declared part of a native study situation. A
definition can bound initial hunger, thirst and fatigue from zero through one.
The loaded study deterministically samples each person, writes the real native
stat once after their body exists and persists the application receipt. Later
ticks and reloads preserve changes made by ordinary physiology and action.

The situation remains provenance. It cannot name a response, expected behavior,
preferred model or teaching target. Package validation rejects unknown need
names, extra fields, out-of-range values and inverted ranges. Observation and
session records remain `datasetAdmission: unreviewed` with a null behavioral
verdict.

The first pressure study used the existing six-person Echo Creek house and
bounded hunger and thirst. All six native bodies remained alive through a normal
checkpoint. The live inspector observed a natural provisioning matter, delivered
responses, accepted commitments, exact SourceUse acquisition and advancement
into carrying; one participant entered native Cooking. It also observed weak
IDLE/ROAM reasons, a failed source action and blocked downstream work. The run
therefore establishes reachability of pressure into cooperation while locating
the larger deficit in maintained purpose, competence-sensitive planning and
completion across world objects. It does not establish behavioral quality.

The next implementation boundary is a general person-private procedural
cognition substrate: grounded knowledge, affordances, maintained purposes,
candidate plans, time and upkeep, social feasibility and exact outcome evidence.
The two existing models retain their current ontology and independently reason
over that substrate; neither model owns it. Lifecycle and reproduction can then
use the same durable intention and native-result chain rather than becoming
scripted events.
