## Record

The meeting became a moment. The engine's own social chemistry (intoxication,
pain, panic, stress, anger, morale) colors every encounter through bounded
multipliers - drink loosens both ways, pain and stress shorten the fuse, low
morale narrows the warm channels - temperament still deciding the shape while
state decides the weather; and the out-of-model honesty line was written
rather than fudged. Separately, the county's radio bulletins stopped being
player-only narration: when the wire airs, the claims behind what was actually
read out are delivered to every living survivor who really owns a receiver,
written at told weight - a death lands with its handler and a feud or peace
moves stances; vanilla's authored broadcasts remain prose because parsing
narration into beliefs would be inventing knowledge, and that refusal is
stated.
