## Record

Composition with the world and judgment of the work. Population exchange: the
operator's directive landed - a survivor's placement takes back one of the
dead within a short reach of their ground (the population is unchanged; one of
the dead is simply not dead in this county), honest degradation when no corpse
is convenient, deliberately scoped so neighborhoods are not quietly cleared.
The porch: playing music is a real sound that carries its honest price, and
idle housemates come over because co-location is what every meeting has always
run on; listeners gain real engine morale, the player is a listener like
anyone, and no musicianship skill is invented because vanilla has none. The
capability survey: items and vehicles are discovered from the live script
registry by the categories content uses to describe itself - the county never
learns the word RV, it learns this world contains something that carries six
- with the two name-bound cases admitted in their own comments. Evidence-based
judging: work is judged by the state it leaves rather than by attribution -
the medic when the house still carries fouled dressings, the cook when raw
danger sits in the larder - with quiet nights and thin shelves deliberately
unjudged (a measure that cannot tell bad work from a bad world is worse than
none), one revocation per election, and the pcall-blindness catch recorded
(an unjudgeable cook keeps the job rather than being condemned by our own
blindness).
