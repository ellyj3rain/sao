## Record

The options screen became legible and the engine's runtime got its first law
from play. Labels now carry the unit, the meaning of every sentinel, and the
reasonable range, because a tooltip needs hovering and the county's most
consequential option looked like "off"; a near-shipped inversion on the
death-refill label was caught by reading the code before writing the label.
The engine's line-break idiom was learned from its own translation files and
the tooltips normalized to it. Then the play crash: `next` is not callable in
this engine's Lua - Kahlua's BaseLib registers a subset, verified by
disassembly, and the crash came from a live game, not a search. The session's
UI flicker was traced to a hand-edited foreign workshop file, not this mod,
and noted where the owner of the fix lives.
