## Record

The founding law was enforced on the founder. The operator's correction, three
escalations deep and made structural law: "We're making a simulation... what
they're around does matter and who they are does matter as to what they
spot." Every convenience table then died: possessions derive at first
materialization - the real containers around the life they lived (place
provides), filtered by what that person would notice (person spots), with
ownership read from what the world actually yielded. The material loop closed
around it: forager sweeps collect from real containers (never off their own
shelves), hauls shelve through the sanctioned deposit, and bread that walks to
an ally now came off a shelf somewhere. The quartermaster reads the larder
into a claim that ages out honestly (stale claims render nothing); lean houses
stretch their sweeps and say so; full larders open hands at the margin; and
autumn re-reads the same count against the winter ahead without duplicating
logic. The dead gained their own epistemics: fearful tellers presume the
long-unseen hurt dead at told weight, the reunion moment corrects them with
the teller paying for bad word, and nobody is a liar by flag. Then the
ventures completed their arc into a feature-complex that runs through B1: the
search for the absent, fired by worry that is felt rather than scheduled -
patience computed per person from what was actually announced, learned
expectations from watched returns, recursion damped structurally - "you don't
act because you see and hear; you act because you feel and need".
