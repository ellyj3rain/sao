house reads its own counted claims for unmet needs (a real wound and no medic,
a lean larder or dark hearth and no forager, a war and no watch) and promotes
its best-suited member into the gap, skill first and class as tiebreak, the
chair's word never overridden, one promotion per election: houses change a
person at a time, the same way they come apart. The thread index was brought
current in the same pass - it had not been touched since the era opened, and
