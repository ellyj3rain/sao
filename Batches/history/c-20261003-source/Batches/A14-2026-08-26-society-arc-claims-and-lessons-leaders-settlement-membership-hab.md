## Record

The operator ratified survivors as true agentic actors with independent
organization, governance, occupied territory, a clock that shapes knowledge,
lessons from the dead, player-joinable politics, habit realism, and bonds that
break (DR-006), and the reference implementation's social systems were mapped
end to end for translation grounds. Seven gates were built in one stretch:
S1 - the apocalypse clock and a past expressed as a small set of settled claims
with provenance (the generated backstory was corrected into claims on the
operator's review - text renders at read time, nothing stores prose, and at
most one claim carries a dead person's name); S2 - deaths mint lessons and the
lessons transmit one per conversation, echoing into the trait primitive (the
same provenance ladder as everywhere); S3 - leaders as trust-sum facts with
real consumers (anchoring, refuge, consolidation, succession at death); S4 -
companies name themselves, scout scored buildings, and claim them by standing
in them; S5 - the player among them through three plain verbs riding the same
standing web; S6 - habits on audited surfaces (smoking is engine-real; bathing
has no safe NPC surface and none was invented); S7 - one bond per person over
real tenure, deepening four behaviors, and the trauma fork on losing it -
divergent by who the survivor already was. Epistemics uniform per the claims
law throughout.
