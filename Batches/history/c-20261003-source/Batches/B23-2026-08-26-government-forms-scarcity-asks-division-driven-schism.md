## Record

Governance took its complete shapes. The deputy line: a house can have a
second. The turn: a house's creed can turn on a margin, never a mood. The five
forms, from the operator: empty (nobody has earned the right to speak),
ladder, council, divided (two creeds under one roof until the room splits -
whose word reaches you is decided by who you lean with), and flight (not a
government at all: the larder, water and hearth have failed, and it is checked
first because it is terminal); the cult falls out for free; and the influence
verbs let an accepted insider urge council or a deputy - never a split, never
a flight, and not to a house already breaking. Division then gained its
"until": the quarrel bends trust toward its two faces until the existing
schism machinery fires along the fault line, with the mirror's
assert-something-trivially-true verdict killed and the genuine structural wall
(housemates could never become hostile through politics) fixed so the chain
completes. The ask: a lean house can say so on the wire, and who answers is
each creed's own already-declared policy - mercy and road answer out of
surplus, wall does not, that is what wall means - the gift kept a gift, the
debt ledger remembering who fed you, no price anywhere invented. And the
county became visible without management screens: the window reads its own
data honestly rather than posing a question.
