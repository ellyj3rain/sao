## Record

DR-036 makes the day-zero start the case this mod is for and the
generator everything else runs on. Against that, an audit of what
the day-zero switch actually reaches found it changes two things:
no lessons are seeded, and only the duty trades go armed. Everything
else about the county was identical before and after the world
ended.

**The question nothing was asking.** The county has written three
stamps since [B1] and [B3] - the first of them seen to kill, the
dead not staying dead, the taps running dry - and read them in
exactly two places, both of which only print. Not one decision in
the tree consulted them. So a household of three in a working world
posted a sentry on its first night, and people crossed town for a
better weapon on an ordinary Tuesday, because nothing in the county
knew the difference.

**`fallHasCome`, derived twice over.** The county can be asked now,
and the answer never comes off the sandbox dial - a switch says how
a world STARTED, and this asks what the county knows. Either of two
things makes it true. The calendar: the record's own day has come
([C36] put the county on that calendar), so a world beginning on or
after the day the fall began is a fallen world from its first
minute, whatever anybody here has personally seen. Or the county:
somebody here saw it, which is what makes a world that begins
BEFORE that day an ordinary county until its own first horror. That
second clause is the day-zero start watching itself happen.

**What now waits for it.** The night watch - nobody sits up in a
world where nothing has gone wrong, and a watch is something people
start keeping after a reason to. The journey for a better weapon and
the journey for ammunition - a civilian does not cross town armed
shopping while the world still works, and what they already carry
they carry. And scouting somewhere defensible to live: people have
homes until the day they need walls.

**What deliberately does not wait for it.** Eating, drinking, warmth,
treating a wound, mourning, going home at dusk, looking for someone
who has not come back, meeting people on the road, falling out and
making up. Those are not survival behaviours, they are what people
do. The watch designation already waited on a feud existing and was
correct before this batch.

**Border 116** holds the question and each of its four uses, and
holds the thing that matters most about it: that the answer is
derived from the record and the county's own stamps, and that the
sandbox switch is not consulted anywhere in it. A dial-driven answer
would look identical in play and would be a different mod - it would
mean the county behaves the way a setting says rather than the way
its own knowledge does. Its control is the pre-batch tree, where
nothing asks at all.

**Not in this batch.** The rest of the day-zero arc: normal-life
behaviour on open streets, the outbreak propagating through the
engine's own infection and turning, the chaos made legible, ventures
socialized, vehicles as composition. Running the same machinery
forward for a save that starts years out. The communication pipeline.

This governed record is the portable project history for this unit.
