## Record

SPEECH_ML_DESIGN.md Decision 4, ratified through Crucible on
2026-08-29 and called there the hardest single piece in the design:
no-invention is enforced by constrained decoding, not by instruction.
The speaker composes sentences freely, but every fact position can
only be filled from that person's actual memories - it physically
lacks a vocabulary for a brother who does not exist. The same
ratification fixed how it is proved: no emitted sentence may assert a
fact absent from the input claim set, verified mechanically over test
corpora, not by review.

**Built before the model, on purpose.** There is no speaker and no
trained anything. The fence is what makes a wrong model harmless, so
it exists and is proved on its own; whatever arrives later is handed a
vocabulary it cannot escape rather than being asked to behave. This is
also the piece that needed nothing else - no corpus, no budget
measurement, no data pipeline - while the corpus waits on sourcing and
the training waits on a budget only the game can measure.

**The slots are not invented.** A slot is a field of the knowledge
surface's own facts, and the values permitted in it are the values
that field actually has in this person's claims. Nobody wrote a
taxonomy: the fence is the claim set read sideways. Border 120 holds
that literally, by refusing any hardcoded slot name in the fence
itself.

**Exact, never fuzzy, and never advisory.** A name one letter
different is a different person, so the comparison is exact after
trimming. A slot the person holds nothing in cannot be filled at all,
and an unknown slot is not a permissive default. There is no warning
path and no "should": a value is permitted or it is not, and a fence
that throws refuses rather than permits.

**Border 120 proves it over a corpus, off the game.** True fillings
pass; invented ones are refused; and so are the near-misses, which are
the only interesting case - a name one letter off, a different case, a
count larger than the one they saw, a real value in the wrong slot, a
person's name offered as a leader's. A person who knows nothing can
say nothing, and every filling violates their empty fence. Malformed
input opens no holes.

**It reaches a real person.** The knowledge surface renders a claim
set flat, taking every word and number it holds under the field it
came from and inventing nothing; the bridge fences it; and the panel
says how much a person could say at all, which is a number that grows
as they learn things.

**Three instrument errors, mine, caught by the border.** The expected
slot count was wrong; a prose check spanned a line break; and then the
same check spanned a comment's continuation asterisk. Each time the
code was right and the reader was not, which is the failure this
repository has recorded most often.

**Not in this batch.** The speaker itself, and the understander. The
rule floors of Decision 5, which wait on a speaker to hold them. The
sentence templates a decoder would fill - the fence says what may go
in a position, not what the positions are. The budget measurement,
whose instrument has existed since [C28] and needs the game. The seed
corpus, which waits on sourcing decisions that are the operator's.

This governed record is the portable project history for this unit.
