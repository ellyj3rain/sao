## Record

DR-033 (the operator's direction of 2026-09-06, paraphrased) refined
the talking system in three places: the register follows the strain,
orders follow standing, and the county runs without the engine. The
three forks it left went to Crucible the same day and came back
without a rule to author - command as CAO's Authority pillar does it
on the Standing that exists, refusal contextual, the engineless
county unscoped since the game is loaded anyway - so the register is
the one part that builds, and this batch builds it on the substrate
[C27] laid.

**What the surface carries now.** `SAO_Knowledge.conditioning` reads
the strain beside the axes, trust and the moment the design ratified
(SPEECH_ML_DESIGN.md, Decision 5): the *situation*, taken from the
controller's own pressure answer and state - under threat when the
agent is engaging, alert or fleeing; working when the pressure is a
designation; resting when it is a chosen rest - and whether the
speaker is *spent*, read off the body's own stats (tired past six
tenths or under three tenths of stamina; the bar is ours and says
so). Nothing where there is no live agent or body: a reader that
cannot tell says nothing, and it still writes nothing (the one-loop
law, [B27]).

**What the design says.** Decision 5 is amended: the situation and
the energy are inputs beside war, grief, debt and the bite, and the
rule floors shorten the register under strain - under threat one
short sentence and no asking back; at work at most two and only what
the work needs; spent, no chat. The seed corpus must show the same
person in both registers; the floors are bordered when the speaker
exists to hold them. Speakeasy's voice material carries the same
rule.

**Border 101** now asks the stub county for both: p1 has a live
agent under threat and a body spent, and the surface answers "under
threat" and true. The probe's body is a bare table, so every engine
call on it throws into a pcall - the surface's own law, exercised.

**Not in this batch.** The floors themselves (no speaker exists yet);
the order as an utterance the understander reads (the command arc,
unscheduled); any change to what survivors say to each other through
`SAO_Voice`, whose line tables are the placeholder the design
replaces.

This governed record is the portable project history for this unit.
