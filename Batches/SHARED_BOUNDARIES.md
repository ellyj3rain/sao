## Integration order

Changes first establish identity, time, current physical state and private evidence. Planning and social appraisal consume those contracts; Standing and native owners admit and execute selected actions. Measured results return to memory, learning and durable social state. Model capture/export and observation read those boundaries with their own evidence horizons.

D can work independently within a boundary after its required inputs and output meaning are stable. Cross-boundary work names the producer and consumer together, with persistence and a discriminating verification case. Acceptance remains scoped to actual delivered behavior.
