## Measured contract

Speakeasy Record 54 corrected evidence binding but still lacked an immutable
conversation input. C75 assigned references to a supplied snapshot label and
silently retained partial answers after source-reader failures. The started
knowledge topic also read county-wide chronicle stamps as personal knowledge.
These defects prevented the reference from proving what one person could know.

## Implemented behavior

Knowledge now offers an evidence-aware catalogue path. It records invoked
readers, requested topics and failures, and refuses missing, unsupported or
uninitialized Standing, WorldSources and WorldKnowledge owners before collecting
facts. Ordinary inspection retains partial-answer compatibility. Evidence reads
validate world-claim return shape and request strict radio-receipt reading.
Malformed saved reception evidence cannot disappear as an empty history.

The started topic reads this person's actual radio receipts instead of the
county-wide chronicle. A report preserves its broadcast, teller and reception
hour. Heard dates remain separate from an event date that the broadcast did not
supply. Existing personal first-lesson knowledge remains available.

The offline capture resolves distinct living participants at the current county
hour, binds the full calendar instant and catalogue, and serializes before
returning. The controlled bodyless host freezes every durable owner plus the
complete private-belief store around the read and rejects mutation or owner
replacement. Loaded body conditioning is explicitly unavailable in this host.
Python binds snapshot and claim references to the canonical observation bytes,
including the authored question, then verifies unchanged source hashes and
publishes the capture and manifest by one directory rename. Existing evidence
cannot be overwritten.

## Concrete result

The controlled example creates Mara Reed and Jon Vale through Identity and
History. Production WorldKnowledge records Mara's initial-resident acquisition
of the July 2 outage and Jon's later arrival. Production Perception records the
explicitly supplied mutual sighting. Mara has eight current catalogue claims;
Jon's later arrival does not acquire the outage. The authored question is
"Do you remember the telephone outage on July 2?" on July 11.

Names, ground coordinates, mutual sighting and clock are controlled scenario
inputs, stated in the manifest. The acquisition, age, conditioning, perception
and catalogue calculations use shipped modules in the installed Kahlua runtime.
This example has a new namespace and is not the Record 52 person, an observed
play session, a naturally selected action or an approved training example.

## Verification and limits

Border 189 runs the production-owner scenario and mutation controls. It covers
source exceptions and soft refusal, absent participants/calendar/beliefs,
unsupported owner schemas, malformed radio memory, personal acquisition,
immutable bytes, no-read mutation, table replacement, clock drift, content
identity, atomic publication and immutable destination refusal. Known-bad source
mutations remove reader-failure recording and the owner-state comparison; each
changes the named refusal into acceptance. The review findings and complete gate
result are recorded under the batch's evidence directory.

Coverage means the complete current Knowledge topic interface was read
successfully under the stated host and supported owner schemas. It does not
certify the entire world-knowledge corpus or implement native loaded-body
capture, typed task validation, asynchronous result revalidation, model training
or conversational quality. Protected prose and source adjudication remain in
Speakeasy. The next step imports this exact capture there, validates an authored
understander frame, and presents its human-readable meaning for operator review.
The retriever target then receives its independent ruling.

The initial full-gate attempt found that its runner classifier did not recognize
unittest's implicit failure exit. Border 189 now exits explicitly from its test
result; the gate-reach check passes for all 187 mirrors. The failed run was
stopped and retained, and the publishing hook checks the corrected tree.

The complete run also exposed Border 112's obsolete expectation that public
chronicle stamps become personal memories. Its fixture now supplies a dated
private radio receipt and the current county clock. The border checks reception
dates, preserves the personal first-lesson date, and requires all public event
facts to remain absent when the person has no reception. The focused border
passes with the corrected personal-knowledge contract.
