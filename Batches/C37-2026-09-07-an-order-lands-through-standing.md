## Record

DR-033 ruled that whether a person does what they are told is a
social fact - the houses, leaders and designations the Standing
pillar keeps, the trust the person holds in the one asking, and the
competence that one has shown - never a control scheme, and that
CAO's Authority pillar is the model: an explicit record of who may
command whom, an order weighed by the giver's standing and the
follower's discipline and opinion of the giver, refusal a visible
event. This batch is the command arc's first slice as the roadmap
scoped it: every ask the player can make of a person lands through
standing, with refusal inside the disposition's envelope.

**The surface.** `SAO_Command` is Standing's (ARCHITECTURE
§Standing: orders, and who may direct whom), in its own file the way
the conditions and the habits are. CAO's check is carried over
whole, its figures cited beside each: the giver's command standing
is the office they hold over the person - the house's leader or the
player seated in its chair at 0.8, its second at 0.65, nobody at
0.4 - shaded by the person's trust in them by half a point either
way (CAO's opinion over 200; trust over 2, the same half point);
the score is standing at 0.55, conformity at 0.25 and discipline at
0.20; at 0.44 the person complies, at 0.34 goes along grudgingly,
under it refuses; the reason is CAO's in CAO's order - thinks
little of the giver (trust at or under -0.15), sees no standing in
them (under 0.45), or will not be told. CAO's own tuning note holds
unchanged: the default person under a peer scores 0.445 and
complies cleanly. Conformity is not one of the county's eight axes;
initiative is, and its own definition - self-starts against waits -
makes the one who waits the one who takes telling, so conformity is
read off it inverted, inside the same envelope.

**The proven hand.** The ruling's competence and background enter in
the currency CAO's ladder already has: where no office holds, a
giver who has shown a hand in the matter is followed like a second.
A fight is shown by kills - more than the follower's, and at least
one - and a job by the census's own perk for it against the
follower's by [B19]'s teaching margin, three levels, which the
controller's teaching now reads from the module so the two cannot
drift. Read live off the player and through the census; what cannot
be read is not shown. Nothing is authored for it: the county already
turned a witnessed kill into trust ([B19]'s respect earned), and
this is the player's side of the same fact.

**The envelope.** Refusal is contextual, as ruled: after the word,
what the person would do at all, asked the way the controller asks it
for their own decisions, with their own reason. A fight: nobody
unarmed, nobody past the fear a child carries ([C31]), and the
disposition's own choice against the threats they believe in.
Ground: Standing's entry law, what they believe the ground to be, and
no walking into what they believe is there and would not face.
Everything else - hold, keep close, walk, board, step out - is inside
anyone's envelope.

**The asks.** Every ask of a person in the county's menus goes
through one wrapper in the harness: the companion's eleven (wait,
keep close, walk, go home, regroup, get unstuck, look at that place,
step out, get in, work as, and the chair's dealt work), the house
rallied by its chair one person at a time with the count of who
stays put said, and the crew taken - whose raw trust line is gone,
the gate being the willingness. A refusal is voiced with its reason
in the log and a grudging yes as itself; a clean yes keeps the line
the ask already had or gains one, since a click that does nothing is
[B33] again. An answer is now seen as well as heard: the player's
reply path reads the same gesture map the murmur path did ([C35]),
so a refusal shakes its head. The debug orders under SAO Debug stay
the operator's own hand, as DR-033 names them. The panel gains a
row - on your word - in plain words.

**Border 111** runs the check in the engine's own VM over the county
