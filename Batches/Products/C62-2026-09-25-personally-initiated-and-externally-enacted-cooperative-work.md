### 20260919-through-C120:C83 — External life coordination

> C83 makes C79's enacted coordination a general service usable by a registered
> external execution owner. Organization remains the sole durable process store.
> Only an addressed recipient who actually acquires the current proposal revision
> can appraise it, and Communication must carry the independently formed response
> back before a revision-bound commitment exists. Missing representation or owner
> evidence defers rather than being converted into incapacity or assent.
> A broadcast request may discover its recipients only at an actual operating
> radio or admitted conversation; that transport atomically adds the exact
> listener as addressed and records reception. Possessing a process identifier
> cannot take that path.

> Accepted provisioning now admits either food or already stored portable water.
> SourceUse still owns the exact source reservation and acquisition, native
> Locomotion owns approach, and Handover owns transfer. A tap is not silently
> turned into cargo: water work waits for an evidenced portable vessel. Accepted
> rendezvous/holding work uses the proposal's bounded destination and completes
> only when Locomotion returns the exact matching arrival receipt and promised
> activity. Queue admission, movement admission and generic message delivery are
> not completion.

> The process retains revisions, addressed participants, receptions, responses,
> dissent, concurrent commitments, route attempts and consumed terminal receipts.
> Pause records the interrupted route; expiration and withdrawal close work
> without erasing its history. After save/reload or loaded/dormant handoff, the
> controller reissues the exact pending route identifier instead of minting a
> second attempt. Completion consumes each acquisition, handover or arrival once.

### 20260919-through-C120:C85 — Private situation contact and enacted work

> C85 makes the C79 coordination contract arise from a person's private life
> instead of only from a prepared matter. Perception retains actual known people
> and last usable contact addresses. Loaded and dormant provisioning pressure can
> open or revise one concrete Organization matter for particular contacts.
> Contact attempts persist independently of proposals: reaching an address is an
> arrival, while actual Communication exchange is required for reception. Each
> recipient answers from their own current knowledge, activity, capability,
> relationships, interests and constraints. Waiting and silence remain
> unanswered; revision, expiry, withdrawal and retry remain explicit.

> A returned acceptance creates a scoped, revision-bound commitment and nothing
> more. Material work enters the existing SourceUse owner, which must complete an
> exact native transfer before carrying exists. Locomotion owns the carrying
> route. Exact arrival makes the item delivery-ready, and Handover owns the final
> native transfer. Queue admission, source reservation, acquisition, arrival and
> delivery are separate outcomes. Completed, interrupted, partial and failed work
> all close through a matching exact-once result, and GraphPersistence preserves
> attempts, responses, current work and consumed receipts across rebind and
> save/reload.

> The joined test found and repairs a real terminal-result defect. When a
> coordinated SourceUse action released or conflicted before transfer,
> WorldSources retained a terminal receipt but omitted it from the result stream
> because no material completed. The Organization commitment could therefore
> remain pending forever. Explicitly commitment-bound terminal results are now
> deliverable and acknowledged once without projecting material state. Unbound
> abandoned attempts remain private.
