### 20260919-through-C120:C86 — Native study worlds

> C86 adds configurable native Project Zomboid study worlds and an autonomous
> observer. Definitions own dimensions in the installed 256-tile cell format,
> ordered native terrain modules, population origins, copied mod inputs and
> observation coverage. Each run owns an exclusive home, cache and save. People
> retain their existing SAO and ZAO execution owners. The example is 512 by 512
> tiles, with native roads and sparse grassland around four population origins.

> The observer has separate view and region-residency references outside actor
> collections, square membership, births and player saves. Infrastructure
> adapters preserve scheduling, distant chunk delivery and native camera access.
> God view uses native filming lighting and tree cutaway without changing actor
> perception or physical trees. Ordinary NPC update and postupdate restore the
> prior global player and camera. SAO and ZAO participant lookups exclude the
> observer marker. Loaded bodies reconcile actual action interruption before
> unload release; failed cancellation or state capture retains ownership to retry.

> Speakeasy selects camera visits from observed movement, activity and awareness,
> with time for individuals, nearby groups and quieter people. Mousecat displays
> native framebuffer pixels and person details and sends camera/time requests.
> Manual movement holds the view until R resumes automatic visits. Camera
> selection changes observation and region residency. Every scenario remains
> unreviewed until operator evaluation and explicit ratification.

### 20260919-through-C120:C87 — Survival observation and continuity

> C87 extends the native study runtime with authored places, bounded process
> supervision, source-owned person inspection and native camera zoom. It repairs
> body retirement, exact-source approach, timed-action ownership and the
> continuation of accepted work and nighttime rest. The installed Mousecat Desktop
> displays native pixels through Speakeasy and replaces its obsolete schematic
> preview. Camera control and observation do not supply survivor decisions.

> Authored residential, service and farm definitions copy native map assets,
> rooms and buildings from an isolated Echo Creek extent. The builder verifies
> intersecting building coverage against the installed map headers. A sealed
> package can verify its copied content offline; it cannot reconstruct omitted
> outside headers. Starting conditions place people amid actual world resources.
> They do not supply completed actions, successful organizations or teaching labels.

> Every launch has a finite wall-time limit, including paused and watched runs.
> The supervisor owns its process through a Windows job created before launch,
> detects stalled producers and fatal failures, and requests a normal native save
> before bounded forced cleanup. Forced exits remain failed runs. Retirement
> removes the exact owned descriptor only after the detached shell is released;
> foreign, rebound and still-pending bodies keep their owners. An available native
> bridge that refuses construction returns before a bare fallback can allocate a
> descriptor and body; the retained person and snapshot remain available for retry.
