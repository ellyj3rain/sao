### 20260919-through-C120:C105 — Resource outcome trials and asynchronous observation

> Native resource trials distinguish an assigned desired outcome from a survivor's
> autonomous purpose. An outcome requests a measured amount of usable privately
> carried stock. It supplies no procedure, technique, skill, recipe, source fact,
> helper response or successful result. The survivor's existing private knowledge,
> Labor assessment, ordinary and opposing interpretations, and native action owners
> determine the means. Knowing a procedure and successfully executing it remains a
> useful mechanical baseline; this batch does not admit live taught-procedure trials.

> `situation.resourceObjectives` carries up to twelve typed requests. Each has an
> id, revision, declared site, regional actor ordinal, category, target and unit,
> with an optional relative game-hour deadline. The study binds one actual living
> represented person near the site. The binding, original source definition,
> experimental issuer and `OperatorDirect` authority persist across body replacement,
> movement, save and death. Missing or dead people cannot select a new identity.
> Repeated admission preserves the same request; changed facts under the same
> revision are refused. Active native attempts retain their owner before revision.
> Binding time uses `boundAtWorldAgeHours`. Admission, deadline, resolution and
> stock checks name their county-hour clock explicitly. These clocks can differ;
> the study receipt does not substitute native world age for planner deadlines.

> `SAO_ProceduralPlanning` retains the target through low bodily pressure. Immediate
> danger, bodily needs and accepted cooperation keep their existing priority.
> Completed native steps and goal satisfaction remain distinct: an exact refill or
> acquisition can complete its work while the larger target remains unmet. Current
> native private inventory is revalidated before satisfaction. Food counts distinct
> usable item identities. Water sums actual clean, nonpoisonous native water-source
> fluid amounts; the unit is the engine's amount rather than liters or vessel count.
> A bounded inventory prefix reports a lower bound. Terminal purpose retirement
> preserves durable request provenance and prevents replay from creating new work.
> Mousecat's existing person feed exposes assignment, checked stock, target,
> deadline and resolution, with unchecked and partial counts distinguished.

### 20260919-through-C120:C106 — Regional study conditions, hydration and timing

> The study authors initial people by existing observation site before native
> population generation. PopulationAdmissions retains identity, history, lived
> presence and social-unit ownership. Authored quotas describe starting conditions;
> actual generated identities and observed bodies determine realized coverage.
> The source-bound admission is persisted and consumed once. This adapter follows
> CAO's authored-start-before-generation ordering and actual-generated-count
> authority. CAO does not supply a Project Zomboid per-site allocator.
> `PopulationRepresentation` retains the actual returned native placement cause
> for these people. Missing causes remain unknown; a historical placement receipt
> does not establish a currently represented body.

> Private resource planning relates native material drink identity to the bodily
> thirst purpose through exact observed hydration, current permission and admitted
> action provenance. A survivor can select an inspected drink for native acquisition
> and subsequently use the existing carried-drink owner. `SAONeeds.hydrationAmount`
> owns the bounded safe-fluid interpretation. `SAOWorldSources` exports that actual
> native amount and taint state with the item revision; complete private inspection
> and source use carry those fields. Native Pop2 contains Cola and Pop contains
> ColaDiet. Both installed beverage definitions reduce thirst and join the existing
> Water, SodaPop, Tea and Coffee predicate. Unknown, poisonous, tainted, empty and
> nonfinite fluids are refused. Generic drink classification grants no hydration
> knowledge. Historical observations lacking the fields require fresh inspection.

> The planner and Labor distinguish carried hydration from clean-water stock.
> Exact admission relates an acquisition's drink material to the existing thirst
> cognition channel without relabeling its native receipt. Source, item, revision,
> reservation, actor and outcome must agree with the native owner. Acquisition does
> not imply bodily relief; the existing native drinking callback measures the actual
> delta. Study water objectives retain the clean native water-source amount contract.
