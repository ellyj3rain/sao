### 20260919-through-C120:C90 — Cooperative procedures and private projections

> C90 makes accepted work durable beyond one shared phase. The Organization
> process owns an acyclic, bounded procedure graph whose steps become available
> only after their dependencies complete. Each author or actual proposal recipient
> owns a separate projection with personal beliefs and an intended next step.
> Private reasoning, perception or disagreement can revise that projection without
> changing material truth or another participant's state.

> Current provisioning and food-delivery proposals carry three terms: acquire the
> material, optionally travel while carrying it, and deliver it. SourceUse's exact
> acquisition receipt completes acquisition for the enacted process and its actor.
> A carrying-route arrival records travel for its mover. SourceUse storage records
> ground delivery for its actor. Handover completion records delivery for its actor
> and direct recipient. Queue admission changes only the actor's attempted step;
> addressing or another person's progress supplies no knowledge.

> The Controller now requests `workPlan` with the commitment actor and receives
> that person's projection. The operator-only `enactedProcedure` surface remains
> separate. GraphPersistence initializes empty procedure owners on old processes
> and infers no historical plan or completion. Save/rebind retains new procedures,
> private divergence and exact receipts in the existing schema-4 process store.

### 20260919-through-C120:C91 — Autonomous cooperative action

> C91 extends the C90 dependency graph from one actor's long-form work into
> cooperation among distinct people. Procedure steps retain domain, role,
> capability, participant capacity, target and posture terms. A person who acquires
> the proposal can accept a compatible role without possessing every capability
> in the whole procedure. Delivered acceptance creates an actor-specific claim;
> filled roles are no longer offered.

> The same contract covers material work and strategic movement, position and
> action. Parallel actor-owned movement or action steps can feed a dependency
> barrier. A step with a participant threshold retains one exact contribution per
> actor and cannot complete early. Locomotion completes claimed movement from its
> arrived route. Cooking carries process, commitment and step identity through
> native acquisition, appliance heat, retrieval and shutdown before publishing
> `cooking:prepared`. Other action owners use the admitted exact-result boundary.

> The actor-authored Coordination API records and transports a generic cooperative
> procedure without choosing for recipients. A failed exact action marks its
> claim, preserves the unfinished enacted step and records a revision need. It
> does not assign a replacement. A changed graph is a new proposal revision;
> participants must acquire and answer it again. Dependency completion remains
> private until acquired through perception or communication.

### 20260919-through-C120:C92 — Natural cooperative formation and posture execution

> C92 connects the C91 cooperative graph to ordinary private pressures. Food
> pressure can form collect-prepare-carry-deliver work among known contacts. The
> person who acquires an item retains its preparation, carrying and delivery
> steps through `sameActorAs`, so a procedure cannot divide one held item among
> abstract roles. The proposal author may commit their own compatible role from
> their private capability evidence; recipients still receive and answer for
> themselves.

> A privately perceived distant threat can form watch plus fallback movement and
> cover work. The fallback vector derives from the author's position and perceived
> threat rather than map truth. The producer admits two through seven people and
> requires one mover for a pair or two movers for larger groups. It does not
> assign agreement, success or a shared world model.

> `SAO_Posture` is the exact watch/cover action owner. The Java orientation owner
> turns the represented body and head toward the procedure target, maintains that
> facing for a bounded interval and reports completion only afterward. Movement,
> combat, timed actions, crossings, body replacement, reset and timeout stop the
> posture. Death reaches the same interrupt owner and drops its body handle. The
> Controller consumes only the matching action, actor, commitment,
> step and native result.
