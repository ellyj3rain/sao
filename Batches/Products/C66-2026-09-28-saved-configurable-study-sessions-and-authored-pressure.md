### 20260919-through-C120:C93 — Durable configurable study sessions

> C93 turns the existing verified native save and resume path into one durable
> study session. Each native process remains a finite attempt. The session keeps
> its attempt duration, accumulated simulated hours, latest world hour, last stop
> reason and exact consumed lifecycle requests outside the save. A normal save can
> continue; a failed or forced attempt cannot.

> The ordinary attempt limit moves from ten minutes to one hour and remains
> configurable from 30 seconds through seven days. Longer studies can use repeated
> saved attempts instead of requiring one unbounded process. Automatic continuation
> is off by default and applies only after a clean wall-time checkpoint. An
> explicit save stays stopped until continued.

> Speakeasy projects the bounded path-free session state into its existing native
> view and accepts only three additional lifecycle requests: save the current
> attempt, configure later attempt duration and automatic continuation, or continue
> a completed save. Mousecat shows Save session or Continue session as the current
> primary action. Duration and automatic continuation remain inside the expandable
> Session settings section. A successor feed replaces its predecessor only after
> the first complete frame is available.

### 20260919-through-C120:C94 — Study situation pressure and loaded cooperation diagnostic

> C94 makes survival pressure a declared part of a native study situation. A
> definition can bound initial hunger, thirst and fatigue from zero through one.
> The loaded study deterministically samples each person, writes the real native
> stat once after their body exists and persists the application receipt. Later
> ticks and reloads preserve changes made by ordinary physiology and action.

> The situation remains provenance. It cannot name a response, expected behavior,
> preferred model or teaching target. Package validation rejects unknown need
> names, extra fields, out-of-range values and inverted ranges. Observation and
> session records remain `datasetAdmission: unreviewed` with a null behavioral
> verdict.

> The first pressure study used the existing six-person Echo Creek house and
> bounded hunger and thirst. All six native bodies remained alive through a normal
> checkpoint. The live inspector observed a natural provisioning matter, delivered
> responses, accepted commitments, exact SourceUse acquisition and advancement
> into carrying; one participant entered native Cooking. It also observed weak
> IDLE/ROAM reasons, a failed source action and blocked downstream work. The run
> therefore establishes reachability of pressure into cooperation while locating
> the larger deficit in maintained purpose, competence-sensitive planning and
> completion across world objects. It does not establish behavioral quality.
