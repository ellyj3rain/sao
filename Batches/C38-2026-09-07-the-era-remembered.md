The Day Zero arc's slice 6: the era remembered - "before" and "the
day it started" carried as lived claims. The county already stamped
its own first horrors once ([B1], [B3]: the first of them seen to
kill, the dead not staying dead, the taps) and chronicled them by

a first lesson with its hour; [C36] put the county on its calendar.
This batch makes those facts sayable, and dates them.

**Before.** The knowledge surface gains the topic: the year a person
was born (the history's own), the war their life put them in
(served, not merely the right age), where they were from, where
home is from here in direction words, and whether the fall has
taught them anything yet - innocent, or hardened by so many
lessons. That last is the innocent-to-hardened arc of slice 3,
visible in talk. The formative claims themselves stay the lessons

horror - the day, the county's date, and what it taught with a name

told provenance, county news aired once; and the record's own first
day, July 9, 1993, for anyone with a radio to have heard it. Every
fact carries source and, where the calendar answers, the date in a
person's words.

world-age hour from the save's own start and [C36]'s arithmetic;
