## Record

DR-027, built. The operator, on the errand dial: people operate
off social structures and social incentives and personal desires
and understanding and awareness - not a permitted radius of twelve.
The same objection stands against the neighbour framework.

The ruling splits one number into two honest things. **The probe is
perception** - how far a body notices what is around it - and it is
named for what it is now (`PERCEPTION_TILES` in the needs module, all
five finders on it) and it is nobody's option. **How far a person
GOES is knowledge and desire**: when the probe is blind, all four
live need branches (food, water, gear, ammunition) reach through a
shared knowledge step for the nearest KNOWN place offering the thing
- `SAO.Places.nearestOffering`, searched nearest-first in expanding
rings from where the walker STANDS, so the cost stops at the closest
hit and the knowledge moves with the person. Commitment is their own
need against horizons DERIVED from the engine's own neighborhood
quantum: half a cell for ordinary living, a cell and a half when
desperation commits them. The `ErrandRadius` sandbox option is
deleted - not as an option, not as copy, not as a read.

**Knowledge earns no exemptions.** A known place flows into the SAME
call-site law a noticed source flows into: watch-first stores, claim
vetoes, the desperation line. And standing inside the chosen place
with the probe still blind answers nil - the knowledge did not pan
out, the walk goes back to roaming instead of re-ordering the same
doorstep forever.

**Knowledge stays honest.** Held knowledge is revalidated at use
(offersNow - a place eaten bare stops being known good), and "there
is none around here" expires after a day, the unit the dormant half
already measures need in, because belief about absence is belief too
and the world refills ([B39]).

**The dormant half walks the same law.** The day's roam reaches the
home neighborhood (the derived comfort horizon, not a dial); need
past patience cuts ahead of curiosity to the nearest known offering,
thirst outranking hunger on the one how-far-to-dying scale both are
measured on; and one barring function (feud ground always, believed
claims until desperation) serves both choosers, because two copies
of a law is how they drift. Arrival-learning, spending, and the
wilderness drift all stand unchanged.

**Two residues named.** The exchange's policy read of the dial was
write-only - read into the table, consumed by nothing - and died
with the dial. The harness's claim-growth reach ("Take this in too")
was BORROWING the dial's name while meaning property law - how far
past its edge a claim may grow in one take - and did not die with
it: it spans the perception probe now, what you can see from your
fence line.

**Border 99** holds the whole ruling: the dial dead everywhere on
the mod surface (Lua scanned with prose stripped - the modules that
killed the dial name it in their comments), the probe named, the
knowledge step in all four branches under the four standing claim
vetoes, the horizons derived, the revalidation and the expiry
present, the dormant cut-ahead and the shared barring law present.
Its control is the pre-batch tree, which faults twelve ways -
including the exact giving-up sentence DR-027 struck: "is hungry but
knows of no food nearby" while the county's own index knew the
grocery two streets over. Border 29's [B39] link inverted (the
dormant reach must now come from the horizons, and the dial may not
return), and the [B39] places-model link followed the claim gate
into the shared barring function.

**Costs stated plainly.** The ring search probes the meta-grid on a
6-tile lattice; a first sweep over a fresh anchor is thousands of
cached-building lookups in one tick (worst, a desperate walker in
wilderness paying a full commit-horizon miss), amortized by the
per-anchor result cache and the day-long absence memory. If play
shows a hitch when a survivor first goes looking, this is where it
lives - a receipt would name it and the sweep can be sliced across
ticks in a follow-up.
