# C69 producer matrix

| Contract | Existing substrate | C69 producer and owner | Persistence | Behavioral consumer | Remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Performed assistance | C66/C67 native transfer observation and private testimony | The accepted told episode preserves actor, operation, item category, event time and location | Existing bounded Perception transfer memory | Eligibility input only | A report supplies no intent, stock total or historical need. |
| Household request | `Standing.callForBread` creates the request; C67 carries it by admitted testimony | Perception stores explicit food category, request/acquisition times, original requester and immediate teller | Existing 64-entry request memory and 96-hour origin lifetime | Opens appraisal for the listener's current household | Water and broader work requests need their own producers. |
| Current household circumstance | Standing owns present group membership | `Standing.groupOf(listener)` must match the privately known request | Read at appraisal; frozen group ID enters provenance | Limits whose request the listener can use | Past membership is not reconstructed. |
| Ground attribution | Existing observed/heard/told faction and place beliefs | The listener's explicit private claim must contain the recorded event location; told claims require a named teller | Claim source, exact bounds and told-claim teller are frozen on the appraisal | Establishes that the reported delivery addressed this household's ground | No complete inventory or historical claim time is inferred. |
| Private response | Disposition owns compassion and relationship-shaped preference | `testimonyAssistanceAppraisal` combines actor trust with the established credibility-scaled testimony weight | Detached appraisal on only the listener's told episode | Existing `reciprocityToward` bends `wouldGiveToStranger` | No trust, debt, settlement or public recognition write. |
| Retelling | C67 transfers the act with immediate-teller provenance | Transfer copying omits appraisals; each later listener must join their own request, claim, membership and relationships | New listener stores an independently dated appraisal or none | Preserves disagreement and chain-specific credibility | A teller's feeling never becomes evidence for the next listener's feeling. |

The appraisal is categorical: the household asked for food and the reported act
stored food on its ground. It does not fabricate a shelf count, measure the
listener's past hunger, or declare that the giver intended assistance.
