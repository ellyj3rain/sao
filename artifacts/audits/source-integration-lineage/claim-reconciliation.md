## Canonical obligation

DR-032 selected concrete age, development, condition and dependency mechanisms from the mod catalogue. Its same-day amendment replaces external runtime requirements with SAO-owned implementations when source reuse is permitted. DR-035 makes the county self-contained. DR-051 states the broader rule directly: admitted prior-art mechanisms belong to SAO and its constituents as fundamental simulation behavior, and an optional external gameplay adapter does not satisfy integration.

The canonical SAO world ontology already defines the obligation through causal participation. No audit, catalogue or implementation ledger may place an asset, verb, animation, state, relationship or consequence outside the world when it participates in behavior SAO models. Provenance and source terms determine whether an implementation is ported, recreated or bound to an engine primitive; they do not determine whether it matters. Ownership requires the relevant world representation, inspectable affordances, usable verbs, body and animation bindings, state transitions and outcomes; how people perceive and understand them; how their knowledge, capacities, needs and relationships shape possible procedures; how the result persists; and how the operator can observe and verify the causal chain. Copying a file, owning only a controller, recognizing a foreign global, activating a Workshop mod or exposing a compatibility adapter does not establish that ownership.

## Where the completion claim exceeded the implementation

The C40-era credits already distinguished selected mechanisms from whole systems. They recorded reference-only sources, systems from which nothing was taken, and explicit exclusions such as condition medications and journals, broader Lifestyle items and skills, and parts of child and elder behavior. Those exclusions do not erase the work that landed. They show that the blanket statement that every credited mod had been ported could only be true for the narrower selected mechanisms, not for total semantic integration of every pertinent contribution.

A second overclaim occurred in the former C122-C123 records. Vehicles, campers, trailers and animal behavior were described as closed. The later C48 implementation audit demonstrated that vehicle access could bypass locked compartments and use stale coordinates, animals could enter human belief rows, and hutch actions could occur without reaching the hutch. C89 then found that horse riding still depended on an unconnected external owner: SAO had no owned mount, dismount, steering, stamina or persisted rider-pair producer, and the adapter read a global that the installed mod did not publish.

These are completion-claim failures, not evidence that the earlier source integration did nothing. The retained implementation is the starting point. Each admitted capability now inherits its actual producer, consumer and evidence boundary; an unresolved producer remains open even when an older batch called the surrounding feature closed.

## Current use

The current external-mod profile is a study loadout, not an ontology. Six loaded entries have recovered, unfulfilled implementation directives: horse life and mobility; mobile household continuity across RV Interior, campers, trailers and Rolling Refuge; and hobby, recreation and social reward from Lifestyle. Eighteen entries expose evidence in canonical SAO domains. N&C Narcotics compares an external implementation with SAO's owned pharmacology, and two libraries support the study runtime. These labels describe provenance and present use. They do not limit what the world requires, and activation gives none of the entries implementation credit.

The machine-readable lineage is `tools/source_integration_contracts.json`. It records recovered directives, known causal surfaces and known gaps as non-exhaustive evidence. `tools/source_integration_gate.py` preserves the Claude-built implementation baseline with concrete producer, consumer and verification evidence and prevents a loaded external entry or isolated controller from being presented as integrated. It also refuses to let the ledger define an ontology boundary.
