## Established direction

The development target is the combined SAO/ZAO simulation and its learned behavior. ZAO materially changes the world, individual capabilities, encounters and resulting social behavior. Its effects belong in ML inputs, data production and evaluation from the outset. Repository separation is compatible with that target. Standalone SAO remains a supported configuration and an experimental foundation; it is not the sole model of the shipping simulation.

Afflicted and Crossed possess equivalent simulation depth to survivors within their respective constraints. Each remains an individual with a history, private knowledge, capabilities, motives, relationships and stakes whose conduct changes over time. This requirement covers ordinary activity, material work, conflict, communication, social organization and fracture, physical expression, and loaded/dormant continuity. Their distinct pathogen, cognitive, bodily and behavioral constraints determine how that depth is expressed. A change of state does not by itself supply a capability, erase individuality or justify reducing a person to a narrow encounter routine. Existing ownership remains: SAO executes living Afflicted people, while ZAO owns their pathogen state and executes Crossed people.

Equivalent depth is an implementation and evaluation obligation. For each capability under review, identify the survivor, Afflicted and Crossed paths and distinguish implemented behavior, a restriction grounded in the applicable state, and missing implementation. A missing executor or model example is outstanding work; it cannot become a permanent exclusion justified by the character category. Constraints must preserve the established distinctions between Afflicted and Crossed rather than apply a generic degradation profile to both.

Governance develops through people with different knowledge, competence, interests, relationships and stakes. Small arrangements can persist; larger and more elaborate institutions can become possible through actual means and coordination. Scale does not determine a mandatory political progression. The context is Knox County in 1993, including retained knowledge and institutions people experienced before the collapse.

Governance descriptions follow arrangements that people actually practice. Authority can overlap, be shared, be conditional, remain contested and fracture. Internal cohesion is expressed through participants and their conduct. Agreement, dependence, fear, attachment and opposition can coexist. Neither an aggregate score nor a fixed government trait may manufacture consent, succession, a feud or a split.

Political acts have physical expression and continue without the player's presence. An election, deliberation, appointment or dispute has real participants, communication, location, duration and consequences. Entering the area reveals the ongoing process and its actual attendance. Visual expression and eventual learned dialogue follow the process rather than decorate a result that has already been silently imposed.

## Proposed mechanism ownership

| Responsibility | Owner and implementation direction |
|---|---|
| Person continuity | SAO identity and existing durable person surfaces retain life history and social identity. ZAO retains its explicit pathogen/brain state and body-ownership transactions. Political office, past relationships and possessions do not silently vanish or confer a new capability because an execution owner changes. |
| Knowledge and competence | Perception/Knowledge admit acquired facts and demonstrated experience; native skills and actual mod effects supply capability. Extend existing evidence readers. Keep personal interests, present capacity, past experience and perceived competence distinguishable. |
| Participation and stakes | Extend Organization/Standing with explicit person-scoped commitments, disputed claims, responsibilities, dependencies and responses where existing records cannot express them. Every field needs a writer, consumer, provenance and migration rule. Keep conflicting interests and selective cooperation representable. |
| Political process | A durable process links its subject, proposals, affected parties, actual participants, communication, place, current phase and observed responses to resulting responsibilities. Decision procedure belongs to the arrangement in use; it is not universally an election. Concurrent offices and procedures may overlap. |
| Execution and visibility | Controller/action owners handle travel, attendance, speaking/listening, work, refusal, departure and enforcement. Existing gesture assets express actual phases. Completed political consequences follow their corresponding actions and responses, not merely queue admission or an animation starting. |
| Offscreen continuation | Dormant simulation advances the same process record using the county clock, grounded access, travel and communication. Loading a cell reconstructs current participants and phase without replaying a completed decision. This does not require every distant body to remain fully rendered. |
| ZAO extension | ZAO contributes changed capabilities, retained cognition, drives, exposures and its domain-owned actions to this simulation. Living responses consume what those people could observe or learn. Afflicted remain living SAO actors with ZAO pathogen ownership; Crossed and other turned retain their distinct ruled mechanisms and one body owner. |
| Learning | Speakeasy owns task construction, training, reference inference and evaluation across the combined simulation. Runtime selection uses evidence-backed available actions and current owner revalidation. ZAO-controlled actors and living actors responding to ZAO both require explicit coverage; their supported tasks can differ with retained ability. |

This proposal extends existing domain owners. It does not introduce a second social planner, universal governance scalar or parallel receipt system. It also does not fix a new model architecture: the ratified learned-task contracts remain, with concrete extensions identified where supported actor/action coverage requires them.

## ML requirements for the combined simulation

One captured situation needs to distinguish world state used to simulate effects, the actor's effective capability, the actor's private beliefs, other participants' separately held beliefs, current commitments, feasible options and the later result. A true pathogen state can affect the body without becoming a diagnosis the person knows or a fact they may tell someone else.

The existing cross-module envelope should retain exact provenance. Task views determine which parts are valid inputs for each actor and learned function. Audit the whole path from join to task input to runtime consumer; an as-of timestamp does not establish epistemic access. Missing ZAO data in an enabled run must remain missing, not become the healthy/absent-extension baseline. Standalone SAO explicitly records that configuration.

| Coverage family | Meaningful comparison |
|---|---|
| Individual continuity | Same person/history and task with changed fatigue, injury, supported drug effect or ZAO-caused capability; distinguish impairment from changed preference and from newly learned facts. |
| Information | Same underlying encounter with direct observation, uncertain report, outdated knowledge or no acquisition. A recipient cannot inherit another person's hidden certainty. |
| Governance | Similar material conditions with different obligations, relationships, procedures and individual responses; include tolerated disagreement, shared authority, contested decisions and actual separation. |
| Work and danger | Ongoing useful activity against changing observed threats and feasible responses, including consequences of ZAO actors. Continue, monitor, interrupt, cooperate, withdraw and resume must be evaluated in context. |
| Afflicted and Crossed depth | Evaluate survivors, Afflicted and Crossed across the same relevant concerns: individual continuity, ordinary activities, work, conflict, communication, relationships, governance and fracture. Available choices and their expression follow each person's capabilities and applicable constraints. Record missing implementations as gaps; a narrow current action set cannot define the intended depth. Preserve distinct actor/state results rather than hiding missing coverage in an aggregate score. |
| Conversation | Requests, testimony and deliberation have actual recipients and consequences; expression uses the speaker's own admissible knowledge and supported current state. Fluent political dialogue is not evidence that an institution exists. |

Evaluate action appropriateness, knowledge fidelity, person continuity, physical completion, social consequences and speech quality separately. Keep source lineage together when partitioning; report combined-simulation coverage explicitly rather than allowing standalone success to stand for it. Existing approved choices retain their approval and conditioning limitations.

| Representation continuity | Carry the same social/work process across loaded and dormant execution, interruption and save/reload. | Enter during a process, leave, return and reopen a save without duplicate decisions, invented attendance or lost dissent. Test disagreement that persists, adapts or separates without a fixed schism trigger. |
| Material and institutional consequences | Extend construction and coordinated action receipts so choices change usable space, obligations and resources. | Means and participation constrain work; interruption remains partial; governance cannot create material success or vice versa. |
| Integrated data and learned execution | Produce narratively legible comparisons from the preceding mechanisms, including living/ZAO interaction and applicable ZAO-controlled choices; extend actual task consumers where required. | Frozen pre-choice inputs, real available options, separate outcome horizons, human judgments, task-specific admission and changed model output reaching a real action/exchange. |

These units are ordered by dependencies, not mandatory serial completion of the entire simulation. A complete bounded mechanism may feed learning while other concerns remain open. The first acceptance set must cover variation across people, arrangements and combined-state conditions; a single barricade scene is insufficient to define the design.

Configuration verification includes standalone SAO, SAO with ZAO disabled, and the combined enabled simulation; optional hobby/drug integrations get present/absent checks against their actual supported scope. Existing-save changes additionally require migration tests. This matrix protects portability while keeping combined behavior central.

Each implementation unit also carries a survivor/Afflicted/Crossed coverage table: mechanism, causal input, applicable constraint, executor, durable result, physical expression and ML evidence. Comparable depth must be demonstrated through behavior and consequences. Identical choices, equal success rates or identical dialogue are not the acceptance criterion. Limited experiments can proceed with explicit gaps, while complete combined-simulation claims require those gaps to be resolved or grounded in an established constraint.
