## CAO is useful prior art, not blanket validation

Several CAO paths already address the exact failure class identified in SAO. The active-threat predicate is particularly useful. However, `AutonomousHomeModule.cs:668` and `:1095` still reject planners who `KnowsAnyThreat`, including remembered active contacts. Thus the improved tactical distinction has not replaced every broad work-suppression condition. We should transfer and test the intended distinction across consumers.

CAO's initial frontier and settlement generators may materialize authored starting conditions. SAO's current life-simulation contract requires later settlement development to follow resources, actions and history. These contexts have different evidence requirements.

Neither the CAO source-contract receipt suite nor the passing SAO perception fixtures establishes loaded-world behavior. The CAO convergence tool explicitly distinguishes source-contract checks and pending operator-runtime evidence. This review did not rerun those suites or claim their old receipts as current execution proof.

## Proposed reformulation

Keep the R contracts and preserved completed evidence. Reformulate the next coherent unit around **perception and competing activity in the physical world**, with combat, material work and command as its consumers. R6's selected-source closure does not establish general sensory fidelity. R7/R8 own action continuity and combat repair; R9 owns development and social producers. R11 captures the resulting decisions. R10b integration and R13-R15 retain their dependencies.

| Sequence | Concrete result | Discriminating evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Native observation | Each survivor receives physically admissible, appropriately resolved information. | Same scene with changed light, obstruction, hearing/vision condition and observed target state; inaccessible facts remain absent. |
| Competing activity | A survivor can continue, monitor, investigate, interrupt or withdraw while an actual job exists. | Hold the job constant and vary the threat's relevant situation; include lost contact, blocked retreat and a danger that passes. Explain current action ownership. |
| Performed construction | An evidenced need leads to a feasible project and native work, with materials, skill and unfinished state retained. | Missing tools/materials, unavailable route, interruption and reload must not create a completed structure; completed work changes usable space. |
| Social coordination | Someone requests or coordinates work through authority others actually recognize or contest. | Undelivered, refused, accepted and interrupted orders produce different records and consequences; an office or roster cannot supply assent. |
| ML evidence | The same physical scenarios yield frozen decision-time inputs, alternatives and subsequent results. | No future state in inputs; missing producers remain explicit; qualitative review shows a legible scene and actual behavioral consequences. |

These are connected acceptance steps, not reserved batch numbers or an instruction to complete all institutional breadth before any model experiment. Speech/data tooling that does not depend on missing producers can continue under the ratified Speakeasy plan. New behavioral training claims need the corresponding source evidence.

## Discussion to settle

Recommended first acceptance sequence: a survivor undertaking useful material work notices a changing threat, judges its relevance, responds to a delivered request where applicable, interrupts when justified, and can later continue or abandon the work for a recorded reason. This exercises the four requested domains without requiring a complete settlement generator.

Two product choices remain for reconvening: select the first meaningful construction/work situation (existing-place repair/fortification is recommended because it can reuse native SAO verbs), and set the breadth of social coordination in that first proof (individual plus small-group responsibility is recommended, while preserving the already planned wider authority work). Do not infer new salience weights, vision radii, political forms or training approvals from this review.

The current canonical roadmap and Speakeasy continuation should be synchronized after the revised direction is settled. In particular, SAO's continuation still points at C77 import while the active Speakeasy tree records Record 62. Historical approvals and their evidence remain unchanged.
