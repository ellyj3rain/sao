## Method and continuity

The operator requested another C recatalogue, pruning and reorganization to
support the next development work across SAO and its relevant sibling projects.
A and B provide inherited structure and methodological precedent. Their catalogues
remain outside this pass's rewrite scope. Their methods and results can be
improved when a later scoped need warrants it.

The established method groups adjacent work by substance and preserves original
records and mappings. A's 200 entries became 29 units; B's 193 became 52; the
2026-09-19 C pass grouped 127 entries into 50. The present C sequence has since
grown to 120. This pass reviews those 120 actual records, including the previously
consolidated portion. It carries forward valid results while sharpening the
criterion for a coherent product outcome.

Each proposed join has a common behavior, contract or causal delivery sequence.
Separate authorities and independently useful outcomes retain visible boundaries.
For example, provisional shadow inference remains separate from enacted
cross-owner coordination, and durable study sessions remain independently
reviewable. Supporting fixes, instruments, review and publication belong inside
the outcome they serve. A shared agent assignment, broad topic or numerical
compression target is insufficient grounds for a join.

The first 70 current records become 50 proposed units; the final 50 become 21.
Seventy proposed units organize historical work; that status does not imply
current full implementation or acceptance. The final group, current C118–C120,
remains open. The count does not convert catalogue presence into capability.
